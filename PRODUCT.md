# ROSTOV REMONT

## Platform
web

## Product and audience
Existing Russian-language renovation company demo for apartment owners in Rostov-on-Don. Visitors browse work, compare services and prices, then contact the company. The user requested a complete visual and structural redesign, with modern, convenient navigation and less generic styling. After the first published revision, they found the result too AI-like; favor direct language and specific work over smooth advertising slogans and decorative studio styling.

## Existing capabilities and constraints
Static HTML, CSS and JavaScript generated by `node tools/build.mjs`. GitHub Pages repository: `trip-prog/rostov-remont`. Preserve all service URLs, the 27 supplied work photographs, three local videos, inline photo viewing, contact links, consent controls, and legal documents. The website is a demo; contact details, reviews and commercial figures are existing sample content, not independently verified business facts. Forms must clearly indicate that data is not sent. Do not invent projects or substitute generated images for actual work. Existing room-assembly illustrations are a separately labeled interactive explanation of stages, never portfolio evidence.

## Brand
ROSTOV REMONT name and a restrained light palette with a brass accent. The redesign may replace layout and typography. Product facts above come from the existing source and README; the user delegates design decisions through the redesign brief.
